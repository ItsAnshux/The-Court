📜 Community Guidelines

The Court ek chhota, close-knit group hai. Ye guidelines is jagah ko sabke liye achha rakhte hain.

Respect sabse pehle

  • Har member ko izzat se treat karo — mazak alag baat hai, personal attack alag.
  • Kisi ki personal jaankari (address, phone number, etc.) bina permission ke share mat karo.

Content jo allowed nahi hai

  • Harassment, threats, ya hate speech.
  • Spam ya baar-baar wahi post repeat karna.
  • Illegal content ya us se related links.
  • Moderator ki banned-words list me jo bhi word ho, wo automatically block ho jayega.

Titles aur roles

  • Titles (King, Queen, Joker, etc.) sirf moderator assign karte hain — apna khud nahi choose kar sakte.
  • Sub-moderators ko limited powers milte hain (posts pin/delete, users ban) — final decision hamesha moderator ka hota hai.

Posts ka lifecycle

  • Normal posts 36 hours ke baad automatically permanently delete ho jaate hain.
  • Pinned posts aur moderator announcements is se exempt hain.

Rules todne par kya hota hai

  • Moderator kisi bhi post ko bina warning ke delete kar sakta hai.
  • Baar-baar rules todne par account ban ho sakta hai — ban permanent hota hai jab tak moderator khud unban na kare.

Koi sawaal ho to moderator se seedha baat karo.
