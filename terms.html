📄 Terms

The Court use karke, aap in cheezon se agree karte ho.

Ye ek closed, invite-based site hai

  • Sirf wahi log use karein jinhe moderator ne link diya hai.
  • Har user apni activity ke liye khud responsible hai.

Moderator ke rights

  • Koi bhi post/comment bina warning ke delete kar sakta hai.
  • Kisi bhi account ko ban ya permanently delete kar sakta hai.
  • Titles assign/hata sakta hai, sub-moderators bana sakta hai.

Content ownership

  • Jo bhi aap post karte ho, uski responsibility aapki hai.
  • Copyrighted ya illegal content post mat karo.

No guarantee

  • Ye ek small personal project hai, koi uptime ya data-loss guarantee nahi hai.
  • Posts 36 hours me automatically delete ho jaate hain — permanent record nahi hai (pinned/announcements ke alawa).

Changes

  • Ye terms kabhi bhi moderator dwara update ho sakte hain.
