🔒 Privacy Policy

The Court ek chhota, closed-group site hai — ye batate hain ki kya data store hota hai aur kyun.

Kya store hota hai

  • Ek browser fingerprint (device/browser signals se banaya gaya) — login ke bina identity track karne ke liye.
  • Aapka IP address — spam/abuse rokne ke liye.
  • Username, title, posts, comments, reactions, follows — jo aap khud create karte ho.
  • Recovery code — sirf aapke account ko naye device pe restore karne ke liye.

Kaun dekh sakta hai

  • Aapke posts/comments/username sabko dikhte hain (public hai, jaisa koi bhi social site).
  • Aapka IP address aur fingerprint sirf moderator/sub-moderator ko admin panel me dikhta hai.
  • Live visitor list (username + IP) sirf moderator dekh sakta hai.

Data kitne der rehta hai

  • Normal posts 36 hours baad automatically permanently delete ho jaate hain.
  • Pinned posts aur announcements tab tak rehte hain jab tak moderator na hataye.
  • Account data (username, follows, etc.) tab tak rehta hai jab tak account delete na ho.

Limitations — honestly bata rahe hain

  • Login system nahi hai, isliye ye ek closed-trust group ke liye design kiya gaya hai, bank-level security nahi hai.
  • VPN/incognito detection best-effort hai, foolproof nahi.

Koi sawaal ho to moderator se seedha baat karo.
