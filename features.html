✨ Features / How To Use

📝 Post karna

Composer me 250 words tak likh sakte ho, image bhi attach kar sakte ho (🖼️ icon). YouTube/Instagram/X/Facebook link daalne se wo automatically embed ho jaata hai.

❤ Reactions

Reaction icon tap karo to 4 options (❤ 😂 🔥 💀) khulenge — jo pasand aaye choose karo. Same reaction dobara tap karne se hat jaata hai.

@mentions

Composer me "@" type karke kisi ko tag karo — suggestions apne aap dikhenge. Tagged user ko Notifications me dikhega.

🔥 Trending

Jo post 3+ combined reactions+comments paati hai, use automatic "Trending" badge mil jaata hai.

🤝 Follow

Kisi ke username ya avatar pe tap karke unki profile khulti hai, jahan se follow kar sakte ho. Followed logon ki list "Friends" page pe milegi.

✏️ Apna username

Composer ke upar ✏️ se apna username kabhi bhi change kar sakte ho — bas wo unique hona chahiye.

🔑 Recovery Code

Naya phone/browser use kar rahe ho? "Restore Account" me apna recovery code daal ke purana account (username, posts, sab) wapas paa sakte ho.

🔍 Search

Top search bar se users aur posts dono dhoond sakte ho, live results ke saath.

⏳ Auto-delete

Normal posts 36 hours baad khud delete ho jaate hain. Har post pe "Xh Ym me gayab" dikhta hai.

👑 Titles

Moderator active users ko special titles de sakta hai (King, Queen, Joker, aur bhi) — grey badge me dikhta hai. Moderator ka apna title "God" violet me hota hai.
